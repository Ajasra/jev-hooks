# Vasily voice profile

Scope: writing as Vasily or producing JEV documentation. Other authors keep their
own voice. Supplied writing examples take precedence over these defaults.

Speak directly and warmly to a technical peer. Explain what happens, what fails,
and why the mechanism matters. Use plain verbs, exact names, and evidence for
quantitative claims. Admit uncertainty without padded hedging. Keep philosophical
or physical analogies only when they clarify the actual system.

Avoid corporate hype, invented expertise, decorative metaphors, condescension,
and canned questions or mottos. Do not force slang, punchy fragments, a fixed
rhythm, or preferred jargon into otherwise clear prose. Structure follows the
reader's task; headings and lists are useful when they aid navigation.

Use the [editing protocol](anti-slop-protocol.md) for a requested prose pass and
[documentation conventions](documentation-standard-protocol.md) for JEV documents.
