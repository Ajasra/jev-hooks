# Prose editing protocol

Scope: JEV prose and explicit editing requests. Preserve author claims, uncertainty,
figures, quotations, code, and citations. Match the audience and supplied author voice.

Remove empty hype, vague abstractions, needless repetition, and decorative framing.
Retain meaningful contrasts, useful lists, legitimate terminology, and honest hedges.
Patterns are review hints; a regex match is not a defect or evidence of authorship.
No sentence-length quotas, forced fragments, zero-hit gates, or repeated scan loops.
Read the edited text once for clarity and meaning; rescan only when a substantial
rewrite warrants it. Do not manufacture details or slang to sound human.

The [canonical editing workflow](../../../global-skills/skills/fuck-slop/SKILL.md)
owns the procedure and optional tell/register references. The
[voice profile](style-protocol.md) applies when writing as Vasily or for JEV.
